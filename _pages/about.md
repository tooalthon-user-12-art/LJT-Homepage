I am a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://lmlc.medium.com/), supervised by [Prof. Junxian He](https://xueshu19.github.io/). My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**, with specific interests in:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
