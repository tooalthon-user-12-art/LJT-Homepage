I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the [HKUST NLP Group](https://github.com/ustnlp), led by [Professor Junxian He](https://github.com/Junxian-He), at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)](https://www.hkust.edu.hk/). My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with particular interests in LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs), and LLM truthfulness and interpretability.

## Academic Background

I graduated from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/) in June 2024 with a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science. During my undergraduate studies, I was advised by [Professor Junxian He](https://github.com/Junxian-He), who later became my PhD supervisor at HKUST.

I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science at HKUST (2024-Present). I received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship awarded by Shanghai Jiao Tong University for my academic achievements.
