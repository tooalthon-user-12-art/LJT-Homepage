## Abstract

This paper proposes SynLogic, a novel framework for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale. Our approach enables large language models to learn complex logical reasoning capabilities by generating high-quality training examples that can be automatically verified. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our method across a range of logical reasoning tasks, achieving state-of-the-art performance on benchmark evaluations.
