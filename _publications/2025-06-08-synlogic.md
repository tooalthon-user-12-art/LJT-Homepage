High-quality logical reasoning data are essential for training large language models (LLMs) to perform well on logic-intensive benchmarks. Existing constructions often rely on human-annotated formulas, limiting scalability.

We present SynLogic, a framework grounded in the framework of formal verification and large language models that automatically synthesizes verifiable reasoning data from limited human annotations. SynLogic generates diverse formulæ and ensures the correctness of each reasoning step through rigorous verification via an external program or an LM as a verifier, achieving high scalability without sacrificing accuracy.

We empirically evaluate our method over a board range of logical reasoning benchmarks to demonstrate the effectiveness of this proposed method.
