Recent large language models (LLMs) have shown remarkable performance on English benchmarks. However, the performance of LLMs on Chinese benchmarks has been lagging behind.

In this paper, we propose C-Eval, a comprehensive Chinese evaluation suite... measuring the problem-solving abilities of Foundation Models... including distinct levels of difficulty... studies across multiple disciplines... And found that... larger models do not necessarily perform better, indicating a significant presence of ... in the training data.

Obtained state-of-the-art performance on the suite... participated in the IJCAI-23 competition...
