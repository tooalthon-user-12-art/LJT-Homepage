Chart understanding is a fundamental problem for vision-language models (VLMs) yet their performance has been bottlenecked by inherent limitations in both perception and reasoning. This paper investigates the perception bottleneck of VLMs for chart understanding, presenting a novel analysis framework that systematically identifies and quantifies the impact of various perceptual limitations.

We first construct a comprehensive diagnostic dataset and propose a three-stage decomposition framework to isolate perceptual errors from higher-level reasoning errors. Our empirical analysis reveals that existing VLMs suffer from significant perceptual miscalibration on chart elements.

Based on these findings, we propose targeted interventions and demonstrate substantial improvements across multiple fine-tuned and prompting configurations.
