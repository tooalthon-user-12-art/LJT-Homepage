## Abstract

Vision-language models (VLMs) have shown remarkable progress in chart understanding tasks. However, we identify a significant perception bottleneck that limits their performance. This paper provides a systematic analysis of this bottleneck, revealing that current VLM architectures struggle to effectively extract and reason about information from complex charts. We propose several architectural improvements to address these limitations.
