## Abstract

Large language models (LLMs) have demonstrated impressive capabilities but often exhibit truthfulness issues. In this paper, we identify a universal truthfulness hyperplane - a fundamental subspace in the model's representation space that governs truthfulness. By analyzing this hyperplane, we develop a novel approach to improve the truthfulness of LLMs across diverse tasks.
