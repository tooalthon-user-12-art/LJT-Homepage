## Abstract

Hallucination remains a critical challenge for large language models. This paper introduces a novel perspective on hallucination mitigation by analyzing the inner representations of LLMs. We show that the sharpness of model representations in-context can serve as an early alert signal for potential hallucinations, enabling more effective mitigation strategies.
